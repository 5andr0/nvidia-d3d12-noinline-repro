Original project-authored Python, HLSL and synthetic fixtures are provided under the accompanying MIT license. The included data/baseline.dxil, data/scalar-amended.dxil and data/out-amended.dxil are normally validated/signed compilations of the authored HLSL sources. They are the only supplied compiled artifacts. The original baseline remains byte-preserved; evidence/cases.json records the optional paired builds and their provenance. No third-party runtime binary, SDK source/header tree, game data, recovered source or disassembled IR is included. Windows, Python and the installed graphics driver supply the runtime dependencies. Microsoft DXC is optional for investigating shader compilation and is not redistributed.
